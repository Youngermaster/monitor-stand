\subsection{Tools}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Soldering iron with a heat-set insert tip (or an old conical tip) at 220--230\,°C.
  \item 4\,mm hex key (M5 heads) and 3\,mm hex key (M4 heads).
  \item A flat steel block or the side of a wrench, to press inserts flush while they cool.
  \item Craft knife for deburring, optional 5.5 and 6.4\,mm drills to clean holes by hand.
\end{itemize}

\begin{nota}
\textbf{Check your inserts.} The holes are 6.4\,mm, the common size for M5 inserts (for example CNC Kitchen
M5). If your inserts ask for a different hole, ream or drill the holes to their size before installing.
\end{nota}
