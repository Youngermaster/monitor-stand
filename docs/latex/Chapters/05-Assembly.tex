\section{Assembly}\label{sec:assembly}

Assemble on a clean table, top-down from the base. Snug every M5 screw by hand with the hex key and stop: PLA+
does not need torque, and over-tightening only crushes the plastic around the insert.

\begin{step}{1 · Feet onto the base}
Stand the base on the table. Push the \piece{right foot} against the right side face so its two holes line up with the
two inserts, and drive two \textbf{M5 × 35} (with washers if you have them) from the outside, through the counterbores on
the foot. Repeat with the \piece{left foot}. The long prong of each foot points to the front, the short one to the back.
\end{step}

\begin{step}{2 · Lower column into the base}
Drop the tenon of the \piece{lower column} into the socket on the base. The column leans forward, towards the
\code{@jmyounghoyos} engraving, and its window faces the sides. Holding it down, tip the base on its back edge and drive
two \textbf{M5 × 16} from underneath through the counterbored holes into the tenon inserts.
\end{step}

\begin{step}{3 · Upper arm onto the column}
Press the half-lap of the \piece{upper arm} against the half-lap of the column: the flat faces touch, the profiles line up
and the arm continues the forward lean. Drive two \textbf{M5 × 30} from the arm's counterbores into the column inserts.
Tighten them alternately.
\end{step}

\begin{step}{4 · VESA plate onto the head}
Put the \piece{VESA plate} on the tilted face of the head with the deep counterbores facing \emph{away} from the head
(towards the monitor). The X arms sit diagonally. Fix it with four \textbf{M5 × 12}. The screw heads end below the
plate surface, so the monitor sits flat on it.
\end{step}

\begin{step}{5 · Monitor onto the plate}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Remove the Samsung stand and any plastic cover over the VESA holes on the back of the monitor.
  \item Decide the orientation (portrait is the default, \cref{sec:orientation}) and hold the monitor against the plate.
        Its four threaded holes line up with either the inner (75\,×\,75) or the outer (100\,×\,100) holes of the plate.
  \item From behind, drive four \textbf{M4 × 14} through the plate into the monitor. The heads sit in the shallow
        counterbores on the back of the plate. Start all four before tightening any.
\end{enumerate}
\end{step}

\begin{step}{6 · Cables and pads}
Run the power and HDMI cables down behind the arm, through the window in the lower column and out through the notch
at the back of the base. Stick the rubber bumpers under the four corners of the base and under the feet.
\end{step}
